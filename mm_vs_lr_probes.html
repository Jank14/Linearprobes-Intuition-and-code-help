MM probe, MM vs LR, transfer, and CCS

A linear probe is one direction d plus a threshold: score = σ(direction · input). Two ways to pick it, both in the notebook: MM (difference of class means; input = x − midpoint) and LR (logistic regression; input = x standardised, no bias). They can reach the same accuracy with different directions — and that difference is the thread through the rest of the session.
